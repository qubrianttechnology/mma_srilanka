02 Your questions

Before you
get started.

New to the sport? Read our beginner guide for a practical first step.

Do I need experience to enquire?

You can contact us at any experience level. Tell us whether you are new to MMA or already train, so you can ask about a suitable club or programme.

How do I find a club near me?

Browse the affiliated club directory and contact a club to confirm its exact venue, training timetable and beginner availability. If your area is not listed, send the federation your city or district.

What are the membership fees and requirements?

Fees and eligibility details are not published here yet. Contact the federation for the current requirements, any fees and the application process for your role.

Can children and young people take part?

A parent or guardian should contact the federation or club to ask about age requirements, supervision and available junior sessions before arranging participation.

Does this form register me as a member?

No. It prepares an enquiry email. You need to press Send in your email app, then confirm the application process with the federation. Membership and event entries require separate confirmation.

How do I join a camp or certification programme?

Check the events page for announcements. If registration details have not been published, email the federation with the programme you are interested in.

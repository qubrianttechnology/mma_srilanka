04 New to MMA?

Your first step
starts here.

You do not need to have your whole pathway planned. Start by finding a club and asking about a session that suits your experience.

  1. Find a local club

    Use the club directory to find contact details, or ask the federation about training in your area.

  2. Ask before you visit

    Confirm beginner availability, age requirements, session times, fees and what clothing or equipment to bring.

  3. Discuss your goals

    Tell the coach about your experience and whether you want to learn the basics or explore a competition pathway.

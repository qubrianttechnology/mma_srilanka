01 About us

More than a sport.
A national movement.

Sri Lanka Mixed Martial Arts Federation (SLMMAF) supports the structured development of MMA across Sri Lanka, from grassroots training to competitive pathways. Our focus is safe training, qualified coaches and officials, strong clubs, and fair competition.

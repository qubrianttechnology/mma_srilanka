A safer future for MMA in Sri Lanka

SLMMAF's development mission brings athlete safety, discipline and sportsmanship together. Structured training and recognized competition standards support athletes as they progress from grassroots participation towards amateur and advanced competition.

Coaching education, referee and judging programmes, seminars and workshops form part of that development approach. Read our vision, mission and commitment.

Connecting affiliated MMA clubs

Clubs and training centres are an important part of Sri Lanka's MMA community. SLMMAF supports club development and cooperation between coaches, athletes and officials.

The affiliated club directory lists approved clubs by country with their public contact details. Club representatives can contact the federation about affiliation.

Developing coaches and officials

Qualified coaches, referees and judges help create a consistent environment for training and competition. SLMMAF's mission includes professional standards, proper coaching methods, athlete safety and fair play.

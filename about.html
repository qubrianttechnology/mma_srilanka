About SLMMAF.
MMA in Sri Lanka.

Sri Lanka Mixed Martial Arts Federation (SLMMAF) is dedicated to the structured development, promotion, education, and advancement of Mixed Martial Arts throughout Sri Lanka.

Also known as the Sri Lanka MMA Federation, SLMMAF brings together athletes, coaches, officials and affiliated clubs. MMA Sri Lanka is the federation's website at mmasrilanka.com.

Our mission is to create a professional and safe pathway for athletes, coaches, referees, judges, clubs, and officials while developing MMA according to recognized competition standards, proper coaching methods, athlete safety, discipline, and sportsmanship.

SLMMAF works to support the growth of Mixed Martial Arts from grassroots and junior development to amateur and advanced competition levels. Through club development, athlete registration, coaching education, referee and judging programs, seminars, workshops, grading systems, and organized championships, we aim to establish a strong and sustainable MMA structure across Sri Lanka.

Mixed Martial Arts combines essential combat disciplines including Boxing, Kickboxing, Muay Thai, Wrestling, Judo, Jiu-Jitsu, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Grappling, and other recognized combat-sport systems. Our programs are designed to develop complete athletes with strong technical ability, physical fitness, discipline, confidence, respect, and competitive experience.

For membership, club affiliation or programme enquiries, contact the federation at info@mmasrilanka.com or +94 72 619 1191. Explore our affiliated clubs and athlete and coaching programmes.

01

Our Vision

To build a strong, respected, professionally managed Mixed Martial Arts community in Sri Lanka and create opportunities for Sri Lankan athletes, coaches, and officials to progress from national development to international-level competition.

02

Our Mission

Our mission is to:

  • Promote and develop Mixed Martial Arts throughout Sri Lanka.
  • Establish professional standards for MMA training and competition.
  • Develop qualified coaches, judges, referees, and officials.
  • Support registered MMA clubs and training centres.
  • Create structured pathways for children, youth, adults, amateur athletes, and competitive fighters.
  • Promote athlete safety, discipline, respect, integrity, and fair play.
  • Organize training programs, seminars, workshops, grading programs, and championships.
  • Identify and develop talented athletes for national and international competition.
  • Strengthen cooperation among MMA clubs, coaches, athletes, officials, and combat-sport communities throughout Sri Lanka.
03

Our Commitment

SLMMAF is committed to building a united and progressive MMA community where athletes can train safely, compete fairly, develop professionally, and represent Sri Lanka with pride.
